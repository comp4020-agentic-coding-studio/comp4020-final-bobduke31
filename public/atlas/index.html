    How it works

    1. I

      Forge

      Pick a continent, a coast or a scatter of islands. The same seed always makes the same world, so a good one is never lost.

    2. II

      Shape

      Raise ranges and peaks, smooth valleys, paint forests and shores, and see it all in Chart or 3D Relief.

    3. III

      Share

      Send an edit link to paint together live, or a view link to let people watch. Each person appears under their own heraldic colour.
