Raise / Lower / Smooth / Mountain / Peak only ever reshape existing land, and Deepen / Shallow only ever reshape existing sea - neither side can turn into the other. Create Land and Paint Sea are the only tools that change the coastline itself. The water surface stays level no matter how deep the seabed gets.

In Oblique view, the mouse wheel zooms in/out centred on the cursor. Dragging on the map itself always paints - use the compass dial and pan trackpad above to look around instead.
