The Isle

A shared island. Pick an area kind and drag on the map to paint it, or pick a line/object kind and draw it with the map's toolbar — it stays for everyone who visits next.

Areas: pick a brush size, then click and drag on the map to paint (eraser restores the original map). Lines and objects: use the map's own toolbar to draw, edit, drag, or remove a shape.
